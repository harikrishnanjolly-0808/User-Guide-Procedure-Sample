# Procedure to Login to the Flipkart Portal and Browse Products

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to log in to your Flipkart account and navigate to the mobile product category.

## Prerequisites
* A system with the **Google Chrome** browser installed.
* A mobile number or email address registered with Flipkart.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Access the Flipkart Portal
1. Open your **Google Chrome** browser.
2. Type `Flipkart` into the address bar or search field and press **Enter**.
3. Verify that the [Flipkart Homepage](https://flipkart.com) opens successfully.
4. ![image alt](https://github.com/harikrishnanjolly-0808/User-Guide-Procedure-Sample/blob/822488006a252fc1431be1034174f9b9fb8c5d7e/Flipkart1.png)
---

### Step 2: Initialize the Login Process
1. Locate the **Login** button on the top navigation bar.
2. Click **Login** to trigger the account dropdown menu or prompt interface.

---

### Step 3: Enter Your Credentials
1. Click inside the input field labeled **Enter Email/Mobile number**.
2. Type your registered mobile number or email address into the field.

---

### Step 4: Request an Authentication OTP
1. Review the phone number entered to verify its accuracy.
2. Click the orange **Request OTP** button to send a one-time password to your device.

---

### Step 5: Verify Your Account
1. Retrieve the 6-digit verification code sent to your mobile phone.
2. Input the numbers into the designated fields.
3. Click the **Verify** button to complete the secure login process.

---

### Step 6: Navigate to the Mobiles Category
1. Once logged in, locate the horizontal category carousel menu.
2. Click on the **Mobiles** icon to open the dedicated smartphone marketplace.

---

### Step 7: Browse Selected Models
1. Scroll through the available inventory or view the promotional banners.
2. Click directly on the specific **mobile phone model card** (e.g., *Motorola Edge 70* or *Realme P4 Power*) to view full specifications, pricing, and purchase configurations.
